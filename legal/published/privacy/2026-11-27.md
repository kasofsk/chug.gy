# Privacy Policy

Chuggy, Inc.

Effective November 27, 2026

Chuggy is an orchestrator for coding agents. You file tickets, agents do the work on machines you run under your own model accounts, and Chuggy keeps the record of what ran and lands the result in your repository.

This policy explains what Chuggy, Inc. (“Chuggy”, “we”) collects when you use chug.gy and the hosted Chuggy service (together, the “Service”), what we do with it, and the choices you have. It does not cover the Chuggy service when you or your organization host it yourselves, or services run by others, such as GitHub or your model provider.

## The short version

- We don’t sell your information, and we don’t share it with anyone for advertising.
- Agents run on your machines under your own model accounts. The credentials for those accounts stay on your machine.
- The record of the work does reach us. Transcripts of agent conversations are stored on our servers, and they can include parts of your source code.
- We look inside that record only to run, secure, support and troubleshoot the Service.
- We share information only with the people in your workspace, the services you connect, and a short list of providers that help us run the Service.
- Wherever you live, you can ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it.

## 1. Information we collect

**Your account.** You sign in with GitHub. GitHub gives us your GitHub user ID, your username and the email address on your GitHub account. We also check which GitHub organizations and app installations you can act for, so that you can connect repositories. If someone invites you to a workspace, they give us your GitHub username and email address first. For your account we don’t ask for a name or a photo.

**Your agreement to our terms.** When you agree to our Terms of Service or to this policy, we record which version you agreed to, when you agreed, and the IP address and browser type the request came from.

**Workspaces and projects.** The workspace’s name, its members and their roles, the repositories you connect, your project settings and configurations, and a log of who took which action.

**Your content.** What you write and upload: tickets, drafts, briefs, epics, images, and your messages to agents in threads.

**The record of agent work.** When an agent works on a ticket or answers in a thread, the runner on your machine sends us the record so that you can review it in the Service. That record is complete. It includes:

- the prompts and the model’s responses, including its reasoning;
- every tool call and its result, which means the contents of files the agent read and the output of commands it ran;
- the agent instruction and settings files in the repository checkout, such as `CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md` and MCP settings;
- the output of checks, summaries and errors;
- the files the agent changed, which we need in order to land the work.

To land work and to read your project’s configuration, we also fetch from the repositories you connect and keep working copies of them.

Because the record is complete, it can contain parts of your source code and anything else an agent read or printed. The runner removes the credentials it was given from what it sends. It cannot recognize other secrets, so a secret in a file an agent reads, or in the output of a command, can end up in a transcript.

**Runners and usage.** For each runner or chat host you register: an identifier, its platform and the agents it can run, its software version, memory readings, the names (never the values) of the credentials it holds, and when it last checked in. For each job and each turn: the model used, token counts, cost, duration, and the usage-limit readings your model account reports. If you turn on machine metrics, the runner also reports your machine’s hostname, uptime, processor count, load, memory and free disk space.

**Credentials.** We store API access tokens only as hashes, and the GitHub tokens from your sign-in in encrypted form. To act on your repositories we use short-lived tokens issued to Chuggy’s GitHub Apps. The credentials for your model account are not sent to us. They stay on your machine.

**Logs.** Our servers and our network provider record requests to the Service: IP address, browser type, the address requested and the time.

**The website.** chug.gy counts visits without cookies and without any third-party analytics service. For each visit it records the page, how far you scrolled, the links you clicked, the site that referred you, any campaign tags in the link, your country, and whether you used a phone, tablet or computer. To count visitors it derives an anonymous number from your IP address and browser type. The number changes every day, and the site stores neither the address nor the browser type. If you request access, we keep what you put in the form: your email address, and your name, GitHub username, interest and note if you give them.

**Messages to us.** If you write to us, we keep the correspondence.

We don’t charge for the Service today. If that changes, a payment processor will handle your payment details and we will update this policy.

## 2. How we use information

We use the information above to:

- run the Service: sign you in, work out what you are allowed to do, send work to your runners, show you the record, and land work in your repositories;
- keep the Service secure and prevent abuse;
- support you, and find and fix problems;
- understand how the Service is used, in counts and measurements such as volumes, durations and failure rates, so that we can plan and improve it;
- tell you about the Service, including changes to it and to this policy;
- meet our legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We don’t use your information for advertising, and we don’t build profiles of you for anyone else.

## 3. Your content and the record of your work

Your tickets, your code and the record of what your agents did are the most sensitive things we hold, so this section says exactly how we treat them.

Our systems process this content automatically to provide the Service. Our people look at it only when they need to, and only for these reasons:

- you ask us for help;
- we are diagnosing a failure or unusual behavior, for example a conversation that ran far longer than normal;
- we are investigating a security problem or a suspected violation of our terms;
- the law requires it.

We may use third-party AI tools, including coding agents, to help with this analysis. Those providers receive the content involved and handle it under their own terms.

We don’t sell your content. We don’t use it for advertising. We don’t use the substance of your work, meaning your code, your tickets and what your agents produce, for anything except providing, securing, supporting and troubleshooting the Service and meeting our legal obligations.

## 4. How we share information

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t share it for targeted advertising. We share information only in the ways below.

**Within your workspace.** Members of a workspace can see its list of people, with their email addresses and GitHub usernames. Members of a project can see its tickets, the record of agent work in it, its project threads and its usage. Among members, a personal chat is visible only to the person who started it. Workspace admins decide who is a member.

**GitHub.** When Chuggy lands work, it creates branches, commits, pull requests and status checks in the repository you connected, and the pull request carries the ticket’s text. Anyone who can see that repository on GitHub can see them.

**Model providers and services you connect.** Your runner sends prompts and code straight to the model provider you chose, for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Amazon Bedrock, Google Vertex AI or Microsoft Foundry, under your own account and that provider’s terms. That exchange does not pass through us. If you connect a cloud account to a project, the short-lived identity tokens we issue for your jobs name your workspace, project, ticket and repository to that provider. The agent software your runner runs, such as Claude Code or Codex, may also send usage data to its maker under the maker’s own policy. If we ever run agent work for you on our own servers, we will tell you first how that differs from what this policy describes.

**Service providers.** A few companies process information for us so that we can run the Service: Cloudflare, which delivers and protects our network traffic, so that traffic passes through its systems; GitHub, for sign-in and for distributing the runner software; the providers of the AI tools described in section 3; and the provider that hosts our company email. Each receives only what its role requires.

**Legal reasons.** We may disclose information when we believe in good faith that the law requires it, such as in response to a subpoena or court order, or when it is necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of Chuggy, our users or the public. If we receive a legal demand for your information, we will tell you before we respond unless the law forbids it or there is an emergency.

**Business changes.** If Chuggy is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of it. We will tell you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

**At your direction.** When you ask us to share something, we will.

We may publish or share statistics that cannot be used to identify you or your workspace, such as the number of tickets run.

## 5. Where information is kept

We store and process information on servers we operate in the United States. If you use the Service from another country, your information is transferred to the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those where you live. Where the law requires safeguards for that transfer, such as standard contractual clauses, we will put them in place.

## 6. How long we keep it

The record of what ran is the point of the Service, so we keep your account, your content and the record of agent work for as long as your workspace exists.

- **Deletion on request.** You can ask us to delete your account, a project or a workspace at any time by writing to privacy@chug.gy. The Service has no delete button for a project that has run work, so we do it for you. We will finish within 30 days, and copies in our backups are overwritten within 90 days after that.
- **Shared workspaces.** If you leave a workspace that others still use, the work you did there stays with that workspace.
- **Records of agreement.** We keep the record that you agreed to our terms and to this policy after your account is deleted, for as long as we may need to show what was agreed.
- **Other places.** Deleting from Chuggy does not remove what is already in your GitHub repositories or with your model provider.
- **Server logs.** We generally keep these for no more than 30 days.
- **Access requests from the website.** We keep these until you ask us to delete them or we no longer need them.

We may keep information longer when the law requires it, or to resolve a dispute or enforce our terms, and only for as long as that reason lasts.

## 7. Security

We protect information with measures suited to its sensitivity: encrypted connections, access tokens stored only as hashes, permissions that keep each workspace’s data to its members, and access by our own people limited to those who need it. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee that ours is. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you without undue delay.

You can help. Keep secrets out of tickets and out of the files and output your agents read. Protect your access tokens. Secure the machines your runners run on, which are under your control and not ours.

## 8. Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

- give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
- correct it;
- delete it;
- stop sending you announcements.

Write to privacy@chug.gy. We will need to confirm that the request comes from you, and we will answer within 30 days. We won’t treat you differently for making a request.

If you use Chuggy in a workspace run by your employer or another organization, that organization controls the workspace’s content. Ask its admins first, and we will help them respond.

### If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland

Chuggy, Inc. is the controller of your account, usage, log and website information. For the content of a workspace, we act as a processor for whoever runs that workspace. We rely on these legal bases:

- **Contract:** to provide the Service you signed up for.
- **Legitimate interests:** to secure, troubleshoot, measure and improve the Service and to communicate with you, balanced against your rights.
- **Consent:** where we ask for it. You can withdraw it at any time.
- **Legal obligation:** where the law requires us to process information.

You also have the right to object to or restrict our processing, to receive your information in a portable form, and to complain to your data protection authority.

### If you are in the United States

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you. Our website does not track you across other sites, so it does not change its behavior in response to “Do Not Track” signals.
